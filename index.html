Three Great Martian Eras

The map is organised by the planet's geologic time scale.

Noachian

~4.1 – 3.7 billion years ago

Heavy bombardment, early valley networks and possible standing water.

Hesperian

~3.7 – 3.0 billion years ago

Widespread volcanism, giant outflow channels and lava plains.

Amazonian

~3.0 billion years ago – today

Cold, dry and dusty, with young volcanoes, ice and wind-shaped dunes.
